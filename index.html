Derived values. Per-capita and per-fleet rates are simple division; the index in chart 01 sets each measure to 100 at 2015. Nothing is modelled or estimated.

Known limitations. The ACT does not publish caravan and camping trips or nights, and is shown as “not published” rather than zero. OpenStreetMap facility tags are too sparse to chart — only site type, which is complete, is encoded. Registration postcode is not residence: the largest concentrations are rental fleet depots. Two reporting periods appear on this page (calendar 2024 and year-ending December 2025) and are never compared directly.
